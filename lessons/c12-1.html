Counting to probability

C12 Applied Probability and Linear Algebra

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Move from counting, C3.4, to probability: equally likely outcomes first.
  • Write P, A, as a number you can compute, not as a vibe.
  • Use complement and additivity on a disjoint pair.
  • Refuse a percentage you cannot define.

Teaching

C12 is applied: hashing/load, debugging stories, a little ML math so you cannot be sold nonsense. We start with counting-to-probability. If outcomes are equally likely, P(A) is |A|/|Ω|. If they are not, you need a model. C12.4.

Language: events, complement, disjoint vs independent, independence is C12.2. I will catch "50-50 because I can think of two things."

Stat 110 or CS 109 notes, selected. Written work. A simulation project later.
