Idempotency and exactly-once lies

C15 Distributed Systems and Data-Intensive Apps

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Define idempotency as doing it twice does not change the outcome.
  • Use an idempotency key on a side-effecting request.
  • Treat exactly-once as a claim that needs a scope.
  • Catch a double-apply design.

Teaching

Retries plus side effects without idempotency is an incident. An idempotency key stored with the result is a pattern. Exactly-once end-to-end is usually a lie; an exactly-once effect with at-least-once delivery plus idempotent handlers is the adult sentence.
