Teaching

Conditional probability is “given this information.” Independence is “this information does not change that probability.” Bayes is the arithmetic of reversing a conditional: you saw a test, you want the probability of the cause. Base rates matter. A rare bug with a noisy alert is mostly false positives. That is an SRE story, not a medical-textbook only story.

You will compute a posterior on a story I write (alerts, flaky tests, a hash collision scare). The exit says: compute a real posterior on a debugging/SRE-style story. We start now and return at the end.

Probability and linear algebra you will use: Bayes on a debugging story, expectation and tails, a few distributions, vectors and matrices, least squares, a dry sentence about embeddings so you cannot be sold nonsense. Not a math degree. Not a deep learning course. Written work plus a small simulation. The exit is a posterior, a transformation you can interpret, and that dry sentence.
