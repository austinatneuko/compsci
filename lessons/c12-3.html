Expectation and variance

C12 Applied Probability and Linear Algebra

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Define expectation as an average according to a distribution.
  • Use linearity, even when dependent, as a tool.
  • Talk about variance as spread; tails as "worse than the mean."
  • Apply to hashing load or retries at a toy level.

Teaching

Expectation is not "what I expect will happen" in English. It is a weighted average of values. Linearity is the gift: E[X+Y] = E[X]+E[Y] without independence. Variance needs more care. Tail intuition: the mean latency is not the p99. C17 will care. Today you meet a heavy tail with a tiny example.

Hashing: balls and bins, expected load, why "just hash it" still clumps. We will compute a small case and mention the birthday bound you counted in C3.
