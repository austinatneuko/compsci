Packet lab

C11 Computer Networking

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Read a packet capture: find DNS, handshake, HTTP.
  • Filter without drowning.
  • Use the capture to explain a slow or failed request.
  • Meet the C11 exit.

Teaching

Wireshark or tcpdump plus Wireshark is the lab. I will give you a pcap or you will capture your own client/server. You find the packets that matter. You do not screenshot the whole window and call it analysis.

Exit: explain a slow API using the network, not the framework. Write a small TCP client/server, already C11.2. This lab is the explain part with evidence.

Filter, find the packets that matter, write a sentence about time or failure backed by the capture. Screenshotting the whole window is not analysis. Exit: network words for a slow API, plus the client and server you already wrote.
