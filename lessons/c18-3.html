Implementation

C18 Capstone

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Implement against the docs. PRs. Review rounds (at least two with me).
  • Keep the Git history readable.
  • Do not silently change the spec; file a doc change.
  • Correctness is a rubric line (1–5).

Teaching

Implementation is a series of PRs I review. At least two rounds of review with me on the project as a whole, plus PR-level comments. The rubric's correctness and judgment lines start here. A 5 is rare. A 1 on correctness is a fail (no 1s to pass).

If the spec is wrong, you change the spec. Hidden scope is how capstones rot.

The capstone is program-complete work after the gate. Requirements, matching architecture and data and API docs, implementation with review, tests and a pipeline and a URL, operability and security and honest performance notes, then an incident I inject and a rubric. Mean at least 3.5 and no 1s. A 5 is rare. Exit: I would let this into a real codebase with notes, not a rewrite. The calendar ending is not a pass.

PRs, at least two rounds on the whole, plus comments. A 1 on correctness fails the rubric. Hidden scope rots the project. Change the spec in the spec. No archive dump at hour 179. Read every assisted line.

Artifacts exist because a team would require them, not because I enjoy documents. Requirements, design, implementation, proof of quality, proof of operation, then a scar from an incident. Rubric lines are separate so a pretty README cannot hide a 1 in correctness. Mean 3.5 and no 1s. I would take it with notes. That is the only compliment this course offers.
