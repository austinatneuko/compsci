C10 Operating Systems and Concurrency

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Use a lock to protect an invariant, not "to make it slow and safe in general."
  • Name the invariant the lock is for.
  • Avoid holding a lock across a long I/O unless you mean it.
  • Know that "just add a lock" fails, wrong grain, deadlock, leftover race.

Teaching

A lock makes a critical section appear atomic relative to other holders of that lock. It does not make the program correct if you locked the wrong data or forgot a path. The invariant, "the counter is accurate," "the list is well-formed", is the design. The lock is the tool.

You will fix the racy counter with a lock. You will also see a program where a lock was added and the race moved. That is the program sentence: why "just add a lock" fails.
