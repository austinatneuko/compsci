Teaching

The heap is the region malloc carves. You ask for n bytes, you get a pointer or NULL. You free exactly once. Double-free and use-after-free are undefined behavior. C9.9. Ownership is the comment and the design: this function allocates and the caller frees, or the opposite, never "someone."
