Styles (and when they are fashion)

C16 Software Architecture and System Design

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Name styles (monolith, modules, events, request/response) as tools.
  • Catch fashion: a new noun for the same box.
  • Pick a style for a prompt and the cost.
  • Do not collect architecture tattoos.

Teaching

Styles are recurring shapes. They become fashion when the cost is not named. Event-driven is useful until you cannot tell why the user saw stale data. A mesh is not a junior P2. We will look at two styles for the same prompt and pick with a cost.

Thumbnail system-design channels are recommended only after DDIA, for interview shape, and only if I say so.

Architecture is decomposition, style as a cost, data ownership, evolution, interview HLD as a separate skill, and design docs someone can attack. A monolith with seams is often correct. Twelve services for a three-table app is fashion. The exit is a forty-five minute design: constraints, one deep dive, what you measure, what you cut. Two docs. A review of P1. Templates without C15 opinions are how people die on the third question.

Ownership and change first. Style second. Interview HLD last, after you have opinions. Docs someone can attack. P1 reviewed in this format so the seams you shipped become a lesson, not a shrine. A monolith you can evolve is a success. A fashion drawing of a product you have not operated is not.
