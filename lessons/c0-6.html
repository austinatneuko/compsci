How we will use AI in this program

C0 Computing Environment

Locked. Because C0.5 Debugging a broken environment is not mastered yet. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • State the program's AI rules in your own words. Closed-resource first, assisted later, never unread output.
  • Know what Independent and Assisted scores mean, and which one Gate A uses.
  • Recognize a few failure modes. Hallucinated APIs, confident wrong explanations, secret leakage, skipped tests.
  • Know when I will send you back to paper and a terminal.
  • Agree, in chat, that opening this page is not permission to use a model on C0 work.

Teaching

This program assumes you will use AI tools. It also assumes they will make you worse if they arrive before the skill exists. The rule is not that AI is cheating, and it is not that AI is the job. The rule is this. Every major skill is assessed twice, closed-resource and AI-open, and the gate cares about Independent unless the exercise is explicitly AI-open. On an AI-open exercise, Assisted still has to show judgment. You catch and reject bad output.

Closed-resource means what it says. No model, no search, no notes if I say none, no phone on the desk for the exam. I am not chasing purity. A junior interview, a broken production box, and a Tuesday morning when the tool is down still require that you can think. If the only way you can write a loop is to prompt for one, you do not have the loop. I will not argue about this. I will test it.

Assisted work is allowed after a skill is Competent without it, or on assignments I mark AI-open. Allowed is not unsupervised. You will read every line you submit. You will be able to explain it. You will run it. If I ask why this type and the answer is the model said, the rating drops and we remediate. I am not grading the model.

What models are bad at, in ways that will specifically hurt you. Inventing library functions that do not exist. Writing tests that assert the implementation instead of the spec. Papering over a type error with any. Deleting your comments and your caution. Leaking whatever you pasted, secrets, proprietary code, other people's homework. A model is fluent. Fluency is not a signal of truth. Treat fluent wrongness as the default until you have checked.

What they are good at, later. Boilerplate you can already write. Generating candidates you can reject. Explaining a stack trace you already understand well enough to catch a lie. Notice the pattern. The tool amplifies a skill that exists. It does not install one. If you are using it as a tutor for a skill I have not passed, you are cheating the future exam, not me.

Cursor, Copilot, Claude. I do not care which brand. I care about the switch. Off until I say on, and on never means unattended. In C0 and most of C1 the switch is off. C4 is where evaluation becomes law. If you have autocomplete that writes whole functions, disable it until then. If that feels slower, good. Slow is the point this month.

I will not claim coursework made you a senior engineer, and I will not claim a chat model made you a mid-level one. I will mark what still requires production experience. You will not hide behind a tool to skip a foundation. If you try, I will stop you and tell you why.

Your move at the end of C0 is not a pledge in the abstract. It is one message in CompSci chat. You can restate the rules, you know Independent is the gate score, and you will keep models off until a skill is Competent or an assignment says otherwise. Then we start C1, or we start it in parallel if C0.3 is already real.

Work

Exercises are listed so you can see the shape of the work. They are not assigned. Do not start them. No solutions on this site.

  1. Closed-resource. In CompSci chat, restate the AI rules in your own words in five sentences or fewer. Include Independent vs Assisted and when a model is allowed.
  2. Give one example, from your own life or from a tool you have already tried, of fluent output that was wrong. If you have never used one, invent a plausible wrong completion for git advice. Do not run it.
  3. Turn off autocomplete that writes code, for this program, until I lift it. Confirm in chat what you turned off.
  4. Do not use a model to write the restatement. If I suspect you did, we redo it live.

Mastery

You can state the rules without notes. You do not use a model on closed work. Independent and Assisted are not the same number. Agreement is demonstrated by behavior over the next month, not by a checkbox on this page.
