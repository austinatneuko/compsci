How we will use AI in this program

60 minutes · Locked

Locked. Because C0.5 Debugging a broken environment is not mastered yet. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

  • State the program's AI rules in your own words.
  • Name Independent and Assisted, and say which one Gate A uses.

Definitions

closed-resource
No model, no search, no notes if I say none.
assisted
Work done with a model after I allow it.
Independent
The score I use when you work with the model off.
Assisted
The score I use when a model is allowed and you still catch bad output.
unread output
Text you submit without reading and running.
Gate A
The job-ready exam battery. It uses Independent.
the switch
Off until I say on. On never means unattended.

The rule

This program assumes you will use AI tools. It also assumes they will make you worse if they arrive before the skill exists.

The rule is not that AI is cheating, and it is not that AI is the job. Every major skill is assessed twice, closed-resource and AI-open, and the gate cares about Independent unless the exercise is explicitly AI-open.

Closed-resource means what it says

No model, no search, no notes if I say none, no phone on the desk for the exam. I am not chasing purity. A junior interview, a broken production box, and a Tuesday morning when the tool is down still require that you can think.

If the only way you can write a loop is to prompt for one, you do not have the loop. I will not argue about this. I will test it.

Do this now

Look at C0.1 through C0.5. Those drills were closed-resource. That is the default.

You can name one drill you already did without a model.

Assisted is allowed, not unsupervised

Assisted work is allowed after a skill is Competent without it, or on assignments I mark AI-open. You will read every line you submit. You will be able to explain it. You will run it.

If I ask why this type and the answer is the model said, the rating drops and we remediate. I am not grading the model.

Do this now

Write one sentence: what you will do before you accept a model's code.

The sentence must include read, run, and explain.

What models are bad at

Inventing library functions that do not exist. Writing tests that assert the implementation instead of the spec. Papering over a type error. Deleting your comments and your caution. Leaking whatever you pasted: secrets, proprietary code, other people's homework.

A model is fluent. Fluency is not a signal of truth. Treat fluent wrongness as the default until you have checked.

Do this now

Give one example, from your life or invented, of fluent output that was wrong. Do not run it.

A concrete wrong sentence, not they make mistakes.

The switch in C0

Cursor, Copilot, Claude. I do not care which brand. I care about the switch. Off until I say on, and on never means unattended. In C0 and most of C1 the switch is off.

If you have autocomplete that writes whole functions, disable it until I lift it. If that feels slower, good. Slow is the point this month.

Do this now

Turn the autocomplete off now. Write the product name and the setting you changed.

A setting you can show me, not a pledge in the abstract.
