Logs and queues

C15 Distributed Systems and Data-Intensive Apps

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Treat a log as the system of record in some designs.
  • Use a queue as async between services, with a consumer you operate.
  • Know backpressure and poison messages as operations problems.
  • Build the small queue/worker the course requires.

Teaching

Logs in the DDIA sense are append-only histories. Queues hand work to a worker. You will operate a small queue/worker system: produce, consume, retry, dead-letter. This is P2-shaped and C17-shaped. A branded log system is optional; a small system you understand is required.

At-least-once delivery is the default honest sentence. Exactly-once is the next module.

Distributed systems here is DDIA, not a required lab course. Failure and time, replication, consistency words, partitioning, transactions across nodes, logs and queues, idempotency, case studies. You will operate a small worker. You will write a design I will try to break. You will have named the happy path first. Exactly-once end-to-end is usually a lie. At-least-once plus an idempotent handler is an adult sentence.
