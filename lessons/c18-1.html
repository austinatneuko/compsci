Requirements and scope

C18 Capstone

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Write product requirements for a serious project: user, problem, non-goals, constraints.
  • Get a yes before you invent a platform.
  • Size it to 180 hours, not to a startup fantasy.
  • This is not a portfolio demo or a weekend hack.

Teaching

The capstone is program-complete work, not job-ready work — you already passed Gate A. You run a serious project the way a team would. Artifact one: product requirements. Who, what success is, what you will not build, constraints (time, one stack, one cloud). I approve. A moving target is how 180 hours become 400 and a rewrite.

What it is not: a tutorial clone, a weekend hack, another todo app. What it is: something with data, users, an operator, and failure.

The capstone is program-complete work after the gate. Requirements, matching architecture and data and API docs, implementation with review, tests and a pipeline and a URL, operability and security and honest performance notes, then an incident I inject and a rubric. Mean at least 3.5 and no 1s. A 5 is rare. Exit: I would let this into a real codebase with notes, not a rewrite. The calendar ending is not a pass.
