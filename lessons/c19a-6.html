Applying and tracking

C19a Professional Readiness A

Locked. Because the prerequisite course is not mastered. You are on C0.1. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Track applications: company, role, date, materials, result, follow-up.
  • Apply only when I say the resume and P1 are evidence.
  • Do not spray 80 copies on day one.
  • Keep the tracker honest; it is how we schedule mocks.

Teaching

The search is typically 3–12 months after the gate and overlaps later phases. CIRR-audited numbers in the program text are modest; we plan as if this takes a while. Applying before the work is evidence wastes the first impression.

A tracker (spreadsheet or similar) is required. I will look at it. “I applied to some places” is not a system. We will talk about referrals and public postings when we are in it, not as a fantasy in C0.

C19b continues through the search: more mocks, take-homes, OSS, HLD/LLD. C19a ends at “ready to apply when the gate is passed,” not at “employed.”

Professional readiness before the gate is evidence and manners, not a grind that replaces P1. Resume that is not a lie. GitHub that runs. Algorithm and debugging reps in interview voice. STAR stories that are yours, including prior career. Explain P1 without slides. A tracker, and no applications until I say the work is evidence. C19b continues through the search with LLD, HLD, take-homes, live review, and an open-source PR. Do not spray eighty copies on day one of C0.

Columns exist even at zero rows. Company, role, date, materials, result, follow-up. The search is long. Applying before evidence wastes a first impression. Agree in chat: no applications until I say. C19b keeps this tracker alive.

This course is easy to fake and I will not let you. Stories must survive follow-ups. The resume must match the repos. The tracker must be honest. Mocks are scored, not vibes. If P1 is not yet evidence, we do not open applications to soothe impatience. The search is long. Wasting a first impression is expensive.
