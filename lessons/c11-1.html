Layers

C11 Computer Networking

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Name layers, application, transport, network, link, as a map, not as a religion.
  • Place HTTP, TCP, IP, Ethernet, or Wi-Fi, on the map.
  • Know what each layer's header is roughly for.
  • Refuse "the OSI seven-layer tattoo" as a substitute for a packet you can read.

Teaching

Networking is how you stop treating HTTP as magic. Layers are a map: your HTTP message is carried in a TCP segment, in an IP packet, on a link. Each hop understands some headers and forwards. Kurose/Ross selected is the text.
