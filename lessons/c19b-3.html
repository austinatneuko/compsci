C19b Professional Readiness B

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Do a take-home I write, under the rules I write (time, AI policy).
  • README, tests, a tradeoff note.
  • Do not overbuild to impress.
  • Review it as if I were the company.

Teaching

Take-homes are a common junior loop. I will write one. You will time-box it. If I say closed, closed. If I say AI-open, you include a short note: what the model did and what you rejected. A long overbuild on a short take-home is a judgment fail.

This is also how you will treat real take-homes: I review before you send when we are in the search.

Readiness through the search: LLD, HLD, take-homes, live review, AI-open judgment, one real open-source PR. Three scored mocks at hire or lean-hire. Resume I would send. P1 and the capstone explainable without slides. Quality over fame on the PR. I approve the target. C19a built the tracker. Use it. Do not invent stories.
