Deadlock and liveness

C10 Operating Systems and Concurrency

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Define deadlock, circular wait, and livelock/starvation as cousins.
  • Use a lock ordering or a single lock as a prevention on a small example.
  • Find a deadlock in a snippet.
  • Do not "timeout and retry" as a first design without saying what you lose.

Teaching

Deadlock: a cycle of waiters. Four Coffman conditions as a checklist, not as a memorization trophy. Prevention: lock order, or fewer locks, or try-lock with a story. Detection is a system's job at scale; we mention it.

Liveness is "something good eventually happens." You can be correct and not live, everyone starves. Interviews like a deadlock drawing. I like a drawing plus a fix that preserves the invariant.
