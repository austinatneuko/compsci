Teaching

C10 is OS and concurrency. Processes and syscalls are the first third of OSTEP's personality. A process is the kernel's abstraction: address space, registers, open file table. A syscall is how you ask; the trap is the mechanism. read() you have said since C-syslit; now you will look at what the kernel does, copy, block, schedule someone else.
