Teaching

A pointer is a value that is an address. *p is the thing at that address. &x is x's address. Arrays decay to pointers in most expressions; that sentence is the first C exam. You will draw boxes: the array cells, the pointer, the arrow.

Pointer arithmetic scales by the size of the pointed-to type. p+1 is not "plus one byte" unless p is a char *. Walking a string as char *is the drill.

Bugs: dereference NULL, dereference a freed pointer, later, walk off the array. These are not "segfaults as a personality." They are illegal addresses. We will use tools, sanitizers, when I assign them.
