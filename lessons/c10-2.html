Objectives

  • Explain virtual memory as illusion + isolation + cache of RAM, at OSTEP height.
  • Say what a page fault is without saying "the program crashed."
  • Connect addresses you printed in C9 to virtual addresses.
  • Refuse a full MMU design course.

Teaching

Every process thinks it owns a large, clean address space. The kernel maps pages to physical frames, or to disk. Isolation is why one process's wild pointer does not, usually, write another process's memory. A page fault is the kernel's chance to fix the map or to send you a signal. It is not automatically a bug. demand paging is a feature.

You will not build an MMU. You will talk about locality, C9.8, as why this is fast enough. You will talk about a leak or a huge allocation as why it is not free.
