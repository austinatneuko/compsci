Teaching

Docker packages a process and its dependencies. An image is the snapshot. A container is a running instance. Works on my machine is the disease. A Dockerfile that pip-installs and starts uvicorn is the first cure. We are not becoming platform engineers. We need enough to run twice: your laptop, CI, a host. Image versus container versus host.

Layers, .dockerignore, copying only what you need. Do not copy .env into the image. Do not latest-tag as a lifestyle without knowing what you deployed. Copy only what you need. Do not copy secrets into the image.
