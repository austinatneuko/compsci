C12 Applied Probability and Linear Algebra

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Recognize Bernoulli/Binomial, a geometric (retries), a normal-as-shape (not as a religion).
  • Know when a Poisson story is the right cartoon.
  • Simulate one distribution in a small project piece.
  • Refuse a named distribution you cannot sample a story for.

Teaching

Distributions you meet: coin, count of coins, time-to-first-success, a bell as an approximation when many small noises add. I will not dump a zoo. I will make you name which model a story is, and what would make it wrong (dependence, changing p, a mixture).

The simulation project starts here or in C12.3: you write a small program, you compare empirical mean to theory, you do not Photoshop a plot to match.

Probability and linear algebra you will use: Bayes on a debugging story, expectation and tails, a few distributions, vectors and matrices, least squares, a dry sentence about embeddings so you cannot be sold nonsense. Not a math degree. Not a deep learning course. Written work plus a small simulation. The exit is a posterior, a transformation you can interpret, and that dry sentence.
