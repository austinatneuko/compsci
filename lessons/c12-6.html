Linear maps and least squares

C12 Applied Probability and Linear Algebra

Locked. Until Gate A. Systems depth and remaining CS wait until you can apply. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Solve a small least-squares problem: what is being minimized.
  • Interpret a linear map as “where the basis goes.”
  • Know that a non-invertible matrix is a collapsed direction, not a moral fail.
  • Connect to “fit a line” without a statistics-major detour.

Teaching

Least squares: you cannot hit every point, you minimize squared error. The normal equations are a matrix sentence. You will do a 3-point line by hand or with a tiny solver you understand. This is the math under “the model learned a weight.”

Linear maps: a matrix takes a vector to a vector. Invertible means you can go back. A projection cannot go back. Pictures from 3B1B, numbers from the text.

Probability and linear algebra you will use: Bayes on a debugging story, expectation and tails, a few distributions, vectors and matrices, least squares, a dry sentence about embeddings so you cannot be sold nonsense. Not a math degree. Not a deep learning course. Written work plus a small simulation. The exit is a posterior, a transformation you can interpret, and that dry sentence.
