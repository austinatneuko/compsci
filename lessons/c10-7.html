Objectives

  • State what a scheduler is, picks the next thread/process.
  • Use response time vs throughput as trade language.
  • Keep this short: no semester of queuing theory.
  • Connect to "the box is idle but my request is slow", queueing.

Teaching

The kernel, or a user-level runtime, picks who runs. FIFO, round-robin, priority. Pictures, not implementations. You need the words because "the CPU is busy" and "my tail latency is bad" can both be scheduling stories, and C17 will add SLOs.

This module is short on purpose. If you want more, it is optional later. We do not delay C10.8 for a scheduler hobby.
