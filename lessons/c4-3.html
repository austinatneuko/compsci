Testing I

C4 Software Construction

Locked. Because the prerequisite course is not mastered. You are on C0.1. You may read so the catalog is a real map. Do not start the exercises or the assigned reading until this lesson is current. Ratings do not move from a URL.

Objectives

  • Treat tests as a habit, not a phase after done.
  • Talk about coverage as evidence, not as a score to game.
  • Write a test that fails for a real bug, then the fix.
  • Know what a tautological test looks like and delete it.

Teaching

C1.7 taught pytest. C4 makes testing a construction practice. You write a spec, a test that could fail, and a body, in some order that still leaves the test able to fail independently of a copied formula. You run the suite before you push. A red suite is not a personality flaw. A quietly deleted test is. I can see it in the diff.

Coverage, line or branch, is a flashlight. Coverage as a score is how people write tautologies. 100% coverage with tautological asserts is a dark room with the lights claimed on. I will look at whether the tests encode the spec, including error paths. I will not give you a gold star for a number from a tool you did not interpret. Do not add an assert True to close a hole.

Google's testing writing is opinionated and mostly right about small tests, fast tests, and not sleeping in tests. We will read a few posts I pick, not the whole internet's testing discourse. Dual-track appears here as a preview. Some assignments you test closed, then you ask a model to write tests and you reject the bad ones. Catching a vacuous test is the point.

Add tests to a course repo I maintain until I say the suite would catch the last bug I injected. Measure coverage once. In chat, interpret a hole. Closed-resource: write tests for a spec I give you, without seeing my implementation. That is the C4 exam skill, practiced now, not for the first time under a clock.

A green suite that cannot fail is worse than a red suite you understand. Construction is a weekly rhythm: a spec or a PR, a review, a correction. The course repo I maintain is the lab.

C4 is the course where other people have to live with your code. Specs, ADTs, tests, PR-quality Git, review, a debugging method, API-shaped errors, a little FP taste, and AI evaluation as law. MIT 6.1020 is the gold statement of the ideas; we do the work in Python. Google engineering practices are a baseline, not scripture. Dual-track work starts here: closed first, then catch the model. If you treat this course as a checklist of tools, you will still write a 400-line main. If you treat it as contracts plus history plus review, P1 becomes possible.

Coverage as a score is how people write tautologies. Coverage as a flashlight is how you notice the error path has no test. I will look at whether the asserts encode the spec. A green suite that cannot fail is worse than a red suite you understand. Do not delete a test to ship. I can see it in the diff.
